Frequently Asked Questions

What if my browser crashes or I close the tab?
Just run the bookmarklet again. It automatically skips customers that were already fully downloaded, so you won't get duplicates.
Why is it asking me to choose a folder?
Files are saved directly to your computer as they download, instead of being held in browser memory. This means there's no size limit — even accounts with tens of thousands of photos will work without issues.
Does this send my data anywhere?
No. Everything runs inside your browser. Files download directly from HouseCallPro's servers to your computer. No third-party servers are involved.
What browser should I use?
Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. These browsers support saving directly to a folder. Safari and Firefox will fall back to ZIP file downloads.
What do I do with the exported folder?
Zip the folder and send it to the person helping with your data migration, or share it via Google Drive or Dropbox.
