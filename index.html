Frequently Asked Questions

What if my browser crashes or I close the tab?
Just run the bookmarklet again. It automatically skips customers that were already fully downloaded, so you won't get duplicates.
Why are there multiple ZIP files?
To prevent your browser from running out of memory. Each ZIP stays under a safe size limit, and a customer's files are always kept together in the same ZIP.
Does this send my data anywhere?
No. Everything runs inside your browser. Files download directly from HouseCallPro's servers to your computer. No third-party servers are involved.
What browser should I use?
Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Avoid Safari — it handles large downloads less reliably.
What do I do with the ZIP files?
Send them to the person helping with your data migration. They'll handle the import into your new system.
