Frequently Asked Questions

What if my browser crashes?
Run it again. Already-downloaded customers are skipped.
Why choose a folder?
Files save directly to disk. No size limit.
Does this send my data anywhere?
No. Everything runs in your browser. No third-party servers.
What browser?
Chrome or Edge. Safari/Firefox fall back to ZIP downloads.
What do I do with the folder?
Zip it and send to your migration contact, or share via Drive/Dropbox.
